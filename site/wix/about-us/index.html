About Us

Copperleaf Cycle Works opened in 2011 in a one-bay garage on Tannery Row. Fifteen years later we’re still a family shop, just with a bigger bay and more stands.

Our mechanics are certified by the Pacific Bicycle Mechanics Guild, and every repair carries our 30-day guarantee. We work on road, mountain, kids’ and cargo bikes, and on most e-bikes.

We’re proud members of the Linden Valley Chamber of Commerce.
